We are writing because the terms of using Hey Maya change on 1 January 2027. Below you will find what changes, an overview of changes in the app from recent months and information about the new version of the Terms.

From 1 January 2027 – subscription

Until 31 December 2026, voice control and teamwork are free for everyone. From 1 January 2027 both features will be available with one annual subscription:

€29.99  / year

In Poland 99.99 zł; outside the euro area the store shows the price in your local currency.

All other features stay free – frame inspections, queen management, treatment, harvests, reports, notes, cloud backup and everything else.

The subscription can be bought in the app: About app → Subscription. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, and the subscription works on all phones with the same Hey Maya account – also when you switch from iPhone to Android or the other way round. It can be cancelled at any time in the store settings.

The free period does not turn into a paid subscription automatically – buying it is your decision. The subscription year starts on the day of purchase.

Changes in the app

Recent versions of the app have added, among other things:
🔳
QR codes on hives
Printing code stickers for selected hives. Scanning a code with the camera – or the voice command “read code” – opens the apiary and hive, just like an NFC tag.
👥
Teamwork
Inviting helpers to your database with read or write rights in selected apiaries and data scopes. Everyone works on their own phone, the data goes into one database.
🐝
Colony state
New colony traits (including “sick” and “drone laying”), icons for colony state and strength in the hive statistics, hive bar icon colour by the most serious problem.
🎙️
Voice control
Photos with “take photo”, a note for the last entry, undo with “undo last save”, opening a hive with an NFC tag.
🍯
Harvests and reports
Harvest summary for each apiary separately, varroa mite drop report, CSV export from eight screens, number of new frames with foundation added to the hive since the start of the season.
☁️
Cloud backup
Protection with a device token, automatic account sync, deleted entries also disappear from the cloud and other phones, and import does not overwrite unsent changes.
